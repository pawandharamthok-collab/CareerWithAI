# CareerWithAI

**Your career, powered by clarity — not fear.**

An honest, no-BS career guidance platform for Indian students and fresh graduates navigating the AI era.

## 🧭 What is this?

CareerWithAI cuts through the noise of AI hype and fear to help students and fresh graduates find their career direction. Built on 18 years of real industry experience in project management, people management, and technology.

### Features

- **Landing Page** — Premium dark-themed site communicating the mission and approach
- **Career Clarity Engine** — A free, interactive 10-question assessment that generates a personalized career report with:
  - Top 3 career path matches with percentage scores
  - Skills to learn and AI tools to master for each path
  - Realistic 6-month timelines
  - Personalized myth debunking based on your biggest concern
  - 3 concrete action items for this week, month, and quarter

### Career Paths Covered
