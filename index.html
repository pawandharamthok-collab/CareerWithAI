The Problem

The AI career conversation
is broken.

The market is selling two things: fear and hype. Neither helps you. Here's what we see happening — and why it needs to change.

😰
The Fear Sellers

"AI will take your job.
Be afraid."

Influencers and "thought leaders" profiting from your anxiety. They need you scared so you buy their course. The truth? AI changes how work is done — it doesn't eliminate the need for people.

🚀
The Hype Sellers

"Learn AI in 30 days.
Earn ₹50 LPA."

Bootcamps and course-mills making impossible promises. They sell you a certificate, not competence. Most students feel scammed after completing these "guaranteed" programs.

🤷
The Actual Gap

Nobody teaches the
middle ground.

Nobody is teaching you how to actually work with AI in your specific career. Not "build a transformer" — but "use AI to be 10x more productive in your actual job."

Reality Check

A Day in the Life
of an AI Professional

What these jobs actually look like on a random Tuesday. Expectations versus reality, and the real salary ranges in India.

💻
AI-Augmented Developer

The Reality

Expectation: Inventing new AI algorithms.

Reality: 60% integrating APIs (OpenAI/Anthropic), 20% debugging prompts, 20% standard software engineering. You are an engineer first, AI wrangler second.

Starting Salary (India): ₹6L - ₹12L

📊
Data Analyst

The Reality

Expectation: Presenting beautiful charts to the CEO.

Reality: 70% cleaning messy data in SQL/Python, 20% explaining to managers why their metric calculation is flawed, 10% actually making the charts.

Starting Salary (India): ₹5L - ₹9L

⚙️
AI/ML Ops Engineer

The Reality

Expectation: Doing cutting-edge AI research.

Reality: Fixing broken Docker containers, managing AWS cloud bills, and figuring out why the model that worked perfectly on your laptop crashes in production.

Starting Salary (India): ₹8L - ₹14L
